How It Works

1. Select and Open

Right-click any folder or desktop item in Finder. Choose "Open iconStiX" from the services menu.

2. Build Your Composition

Add images, shapes, or text. Arrange, resize, rotate, and style objects in the intuitive table view.

3. Attach and Export

Preview, attach the custom icon directly, or export as ICNS, PNG, or TIFF. Save as a template for later.

4. Access Favorites

Keep preferred icons in your favorites library for quick reuse across projects.
